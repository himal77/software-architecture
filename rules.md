# Learning Rules

## Daily Revision Quiz
Before starting each new day's content, a revision quiz of **10–15 questions** covering everything taught from Day 1 up to the previous day.

- Questions span all previous days, not just the most recent one
- Mix of question types:
  - Concept recall ("What is X?")
  - Tradeoff comparison ("When would you choose X over Y?")
  - Failure analysis ("What breaks if...?")
  - Design judgment ("Which would you choose and why?")
- Answer first — corrections come after, not before
- Short score + feedback given after quiz
- Then proceed to the new day's content

---

## Note Format
Every day ends with a saved note: `notes/dayN-topic.md`
Covers: key concepts, design decisions, lessons, case study, concepts internalized.

---

## How Each Chapter Works
1. **Theory** — concept taught anchored to Spring/Kafka/K8s background
2. **Design problem** — build a solution
3. **Critique** — solution gets stress-tested, not just corrected
4. **Case study** — how Netflix/Uber/etc. solved the same problem
